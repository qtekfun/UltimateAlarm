UltimateAlarm is an alarm clock that knows which days are public holidays, asks you the evening before and lets you decide. Everything runs on your phone: alarms are scheduled locally and ring with the screen off, after a reboot and across time changes.

• Flexible schedules: days of the week, shift patterns (4×4, 5×2, alternate weeks or custom), per-day exceptions and vacations
• Public holidays for your country and region, your town's local holidays in Spain (official calendars where published), public ICS calendars and your own holidays; the evening before, a notification asks whether to skip tomorrow's alarms — they ring if you do not answer
• One-time alarms that delete themselves when dismissed, and "skip" per alarm
• Gentle volume ramp, vibration, snooze limits and a backup alarm if you do not react
• Challenges to dismiss: maths, QR/barcode or NFC tag
• 13 original tones, system tones or your own files
• Timers, stopwatch and bedtime reminder
• Backup to a file and automatic backups to your Nextcloud (WebDAV)
• A reliability assistant for your phone's battery settings, and a real test alarm

Works on Android 12 and later, with or without Google services, on any manufacturer. No analytics, no trackers, no Google dependencies. Free software, GPL-3.0.
