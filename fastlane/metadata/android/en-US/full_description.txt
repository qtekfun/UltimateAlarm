UltimateAlarm is an alarm clock that knows which days are public holidays, asks you the evening before and lets you decide. Everything runs on your phone: alarms are scheduled locally and ring with the screen off, after a reboot and across time changes.

• Flexible schedules: days of the week, shift patterns (4 on / 4 off, alternate weeks…), per-day exceptions and vacations
• Public holidays for your country and region, plus your own local holidays; a notification the evening before asks whether to skip
• One-time alarms that delete themselves when dismissed, and "skip tomorrow" per alarm
• Gentle volume ramp, vibration, snooze and a backup alarm if you do not react
• Challenges to dismiss: maths, QR/barcode or NFC tag
• Bundled sounds with free licences, system tones or your own files
• Timer, stopwatch and bedtime reminder
• Backup to a file and automatic backup to your Nextcloud (WebDAV)

Works on Android 12 and later, with or without Google services, on any manufacturer. No analytics, no trackers, no Google dependencies. Free software, GPL-3.0.
